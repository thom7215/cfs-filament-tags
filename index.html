Writing the sticker

A phone browser cannot program these chips. CFS stickers are MIFARE Classic 1K, and browser NFC only writes NDEF tags such as NTAG213. The bytes above are the whole job once you have a writer that speaks MIFARE Classic.

  1. Use a 13.56 MHz MIFARE Classic 1K sticker, sold as F08, S50, or “1K NFC.” NTAG stickers will not work.
  2. Authenticate sector 1 with Key A FFFFFFFFFFFF. That is the factory key on a blank sticker. Leave the sector trailer alone.
  3. Write block 4, block 5, and block 6 with the hex above. Write the same record on both sides of the spool.
  4. Many Samsung phones can do this from an Android MIFARE Classic app. Pixel phones generally cannot. A USB reader such as an ACR122U can.
